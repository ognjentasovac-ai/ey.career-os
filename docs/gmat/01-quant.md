# Quantitative Reasoning — 35 pitanja

Problem Solving, bez kalkulatora. Ciljno vreme: **~2 min po pitanju** (blok od 35 pitanja ≈ 75 min). Rešenja su na dnu.

---

**1.** If x and y are positive integers such that x² − y² = 45, how many ordered pairs (x, y) are possible?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4 (E) 6

**2.** What is the units digit of 7⁸³?
(A) 1 (B) 3 (C) 5 (D) 7 (E) 9

**3.** How many positive divisors does 720 have?
(A) 24 (B) 27 (C) 28 (D) 30 (E) 36

**4.** Pipe A can fill a tank in 6 hours and pipe B can fill it in 9 hours. Drain C can empty the full tank in 12 hours. If the tank is empty and all three are opened simultaneously, how many hours will it take to fill the tank?
(A) 4½ (B) 5 (C) 5 1/7 (D) 5½ (E) 6

**5.** The price of a stock rose by p percent and then fell by p percent, ending 4 percent below its original price. What is p?
(A) 2 (B) 4 (C) 10 (D) 16 (E) 20

**6.** The average (arithmetic mean) of five distinct positive integers is 12. What is the greatest possible value of the largest of these integers?
(A) 40 (B) 44 (C) 46 (D) 50 (E) 55

**7.** A set of seven distinct positive integers has a median of 10 and an average of 12. What is the greatest possible value of the largest integer in the set?
(A) 45 (B) 48 (C) 51 (D) 54 (E) 57

**8.** What is the sum of all values of x that satisfy |x − 3| + |x + 2| = 9?
(A) −4 (B) −1 (C) 0 (D) 1 (E) 5

**9.** Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers rolled is a prime number?
(A) 1/3 (B) 7/18 (C) 5/12 (D) 4/9 (E) 1/2

**10.** A committee of 3 is to be chosen from 5 men and 4 women. How many different committees include at least one woman?
(A) 64 (B) 74 (C) 80 (D) 84 (E) 126

**11.** In how many distinct arrangements of the letters of the word LEVEL are the two L's **not** adjacent?
(A) 9 (B) 12 (C) 15 (D) 18 (E) 24

**12.** Six people are to be seated around a circular table. If two of them, Ana and Marko, refuse to sit next to each other, how many seating arrangements are possible? (Arrangements that differ only by rotation are considered the same.)
(A) 60 (B) 72 (C) 96 (D) 120 (E) 144

**13.** How many liters of pure acid must be added to 40 liters of a 25% acid solution to obtain a 40% acid solution?
(A) 4 (B) 6 (C) 8 (D) 10 (E) 12

**14.** A car travels the first half of a trip's distance at 40 mph and the second half at 60 mph. What is its average speed for the entire trip, in mph?
(A) 45 (B) 48 (C) 50 (D) 52 (E) 55

**15.** Two trains start 300 miles apart and travel toward each other on parallel tracks at 70 mph and 80 mph. After how many hours will they be 75 miles apart **for the second time**?
(A) 1.5 (B) 2 (C) 2.25 (D) 2.5 (E) 3

**16.** If n is a positive integer and n² is divisible by 72, what is the largest positive integer that must divide n?
(A) 6 (B) 12 (C) 24 (D) 36 (E) 72

**17.** What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 100, inclusive, that are divisible by 3 or by 5?
(A) 2,133 (B) 2,318 (C) 2,418 (D) 2,733 (E) 3,048

**18.** If 2ˣ · 4ʸ = 64 and 3ˣ⁻ʸ = 27, what is the value of xy?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4 (E) 6

**19.** The equation x² − 5x + k = 0 has two real roots whose difference is 3. What is k?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 4 (D) 6 (E) 9

**20.** How many nonzero integers n satisfy n² − 3n − 10 < 0?
(A) 4 (B) 5 (C) 6 (D) 7 (E) 8

**21.** The sum of three consecutive even integers is 30 more than the smallest of the three. What is the largest of the three integers?
(A) 12 (B) 14 (C) 16 (D) 18 (E) 20

**22.** If x + 1/x = 5, what is the value of x² + 1/x² ?
(A) 21 (B) 23 (C) 25 (D) 27 (E) 29

**23.** A company's revenue grew by 20% in Year 1. Over Years 1 and 2 combined, revenue grew by 50%. By what percent did revenue grow in Year 2?
(A) 20% (B) 22.5% (C) 25% (D) 27.5% (E) 30%

**24.** A line passes through the points (2, 3) and (6, 11). At what value of y does it cross the y-axis?
(A) −2 (B) −1 (C) 0 (D) 1 (E) 3

**25.** In a sequence, a₁ = 4 and aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + 3 for all n > 1. What is the sum of the first 20 terms?
(A) 600 (B) 620 (C) 640 (D) 650 (E) 700

**26.** $1,000 is invested for one year at an annual interest rate of 10%. How much more does the investment earn if interest is compounded semiannually rather than calculated as simple interest?
(A) $0.50 (B) $1.00 (C) $1.25 (D) $2.50 (E) $5.00

**27.** Six machines, working at the same constant rate, complete a job in 20 hours. How many **additional** machines of the same kind are needed to complete the same job in 8 hours?
(A) 6 (B) 8 (C) 9 (D) 10 (E) 15

**28.** Of 120 students, 70 study French, 50 study Spanish, and 20 study neither. How many study both?
(A) 5 (B) 10 (C) 15 (D) 20 (E) 25

**29.** The ratio of boys to girls in a club is 3 : 5. After 12 more boys join and no one leaves, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. How many girls are in the club?
(A) 80 (B) 84 (C) 90 (D) 96 (E) 100

**30.** S = {4, 6, 8, 10, 12}. Adding which of the following numbers to S would **decrease** the standard deviation of S by the greatest amount?
(A) 8 (B) 4 (C) 6 (D) 12 (E) 20

**31.** What is the greatest integer n such that 6ⁿ is a factor of 20! ?
(A) 4 (B) 5 (C) 6 (D) 7 (E) 8

**32.** How many trailing zeros does 50! have?
(A) 8 (B) 9 (C) 10 (D) 11 (E) 12

**33.** What is the units digit of 3⁴⁵ · 7²³?
(A) 1 (B) 3 (C) 5 (D) 7 (E) 9

**34.** A merchant marks an item up 40% above cost and then sells it at a 15% discount off the marked price. What is the merchant's profit as a percent of cost?
(A) 19% (B) 21% (C) 25% (D) 29% (E) 35%

**35.** f(x) = ax² + bx, where a and b are constants. If f(2) = 10 and f(−1) = −1, what is f(3)?
(A) 19 (B) 20 (C) 21 (D) 23 (E) 25

| 5 | **E** | (1+p)(1−p) = 0,96, pa je p² = 0,04 i p = 0,2 = 20 %. |
| 6 | **D** | Zbir je 60. Ostala četiri broja minimizuješ na 1+2+3+4 = 10, pa je najveći 50. |
| 7 | **A** | Zbir je 84. Medijana (10) je 4. član. Najmanje moguće: 1, 2, 3 ispod, 11 i 12 kao 5. i 6. član. 84 − (1+2+3+10+11+12) = 45. |
| 8 | **D** | Za x ≥ 3: 2x−1 = 9, pa je x = 5. Za x ≤ −2: −2x+1 = 9, pa je x = −4. Za −2 < x < 3 zbir je uvek 5 ≠ 9. Rešenja su 5 i −4, a njihov zbir je 1. |
| 9 | **C** | Prosti zbirovi: 2 (1 način), 3 (2), 5 (4), 7 (6), 11 (2), ukupno 15/36 = 5/12. |

| 20 | **B** | (n−5)(n+2) < 0, pa je −2 < n < 5. To su −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4; bez nule ostaje 5 brojeva. |
