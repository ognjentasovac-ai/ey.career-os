## Data Sufficiency (1–15)

Ponuđeni odgovori su uvek isti:

- **(A)** Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
- **(B)** Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
- **(C)** BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.
- **(D)** EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.
- **(E)** Statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are NOT sufficient.

**1.** Is the integer n even?
(1) n² + n is even.
(2) n² − 1 is odd.

**2.** What is the value of x?
(1) x² − 4x + 3 = 0
(2) x > 2

**3.** Is xy > 0?
(1) x/y > 0
(2) x + y > 0

**4.** What is the average (arithmetic mean) of a, b, and c?
(1) a + 2b + c = 30
(2) 3a + 2b + 3c = 50

**5.** Is the positive integer k divisible by 12?
(1) k is divisible by 4.
(2) k is divisible by 6.

**6.** Is the positive integer k divisible by 24?
(1) k is divisible by 4.
(2) k is divisible by 6.

**7.** A store sold 40 shirts, each priced at either $15 or $25. How many $25 shirts did it sell?
(1) Total revenue from the 40 shirts was $760.
(2) The store sold 8 more $15 shirts than $25 shirts.

**8.** Is |x| < 1?
(1) x² < 1
(2) x³ < 1

**9.** x is a positive integer. What is the remainder when x is divided by 5?
(1) x + 1 is divisible by 5.
(2) When 2x is divided by 5, the remainder is 3.

**10.** The set {3, 7, 9, x, y} consists of five integers. What is the median of the set?
(1) x > 9
(2) y > 9

**11.** What is the area of rectangle R?
(1) The perimeter of R is 28.
(2) The length and width of R are both integers, and the diagonal of R has length 10.

**12.** Is x > 0?
(1) x³ > x
(2) x² > x

**13.** A company has 50 employees. How many have both an MBA and a CPA?
(1) 30 employees have an MBA and 25 have a CPA.
(2) 10 employees have neither an MBA nor a CPA.

**14.** p and q are prime numbers with p < q. What is p + q?
(1) pq = 26
(2) q − p = 11

**15.** Did Jovana's salary increase by more than 10% from 2024 to 2025?
(1) Her salary increased by $6,000 from 2024 to 2025.
(2) Her 2024 salary was more than $50,000.

## Graphics Interpretation (19–22)

Za svako pitanje dopuni **obe** praznine izborom iz ponuđenih opcija.

**19.** *Linijski grafik: godišnji prihod kompanije (u $M)*
`2019: 40 · 2020: 44 · 2021: 50 · 2022: 48 · 2023: 57 · 2024: 63 · 2025: 72`

- The largest year-over-year **percentage** increase in revenue occurred in [ 2020 | 2021 | 2023 | 2025 ].
- The compound annual growth rate of revenue from 2019 to 2025 is closest to [ 5% | 8% | 10% | 12% ].

**20.** *Pie chart: godišnji budžet od $2,4M*
`Salaries 45% · Marketing 20% · R&D 15% · Operations 12% · Other 8%`

- If the marketing budget is cut by 25% and the entire savings are added to R&D (total budget unchanged), R&D's share of the budget becomes [ 15% | 18% | 20% | 22% ].
- Under the original budget, spending on salaries exceeds combined spending on R&D and operations by [ $180,000 | $360,000 | $432,000 | $540,000 ].

**21.** *Scatter plot: 20 prodavnica. x = mesečni budžet za reklamu ($000), y = mesečna prodaja ($000). Regresiona linija: y = 2.5x + 40.*

- According to the regression line, an increase of $8,000 in monthly advertising spending is associated with an increase in monthly sales of [ $8,000 | $20,000 | $40,000 | $48,000 ].
- A store that spends $12,000 per month on advertising and has monthly sales of $65,000 lies [ above | on | below ] the regression line.

**22.** *Histogram: rezultati 200 studenata na ispitu*
`0–49: 20 · 50–59: 30 · 60–69: 55 · 70–79: 55 · 80–89: 30 · 90–100: 10`

- The median score falls in the interval [ 50–59 | 60–69 | 70–79 | 80–89 ].
- The percentage of students who scored at least 70 is [ 35% | 40% | 47.5% | 50% ].

## Multi-Source Reasoning (27–30)

**Tab 1: Email od Procurement-a**
> We need a single supplier for component K-7, starting next month. Requirements: 10,000 units per month (ordered as one monthly order of exactly 10,000 units), delivery lead time of at most 14 days, and a defect rate of at most 2.0%. Please recommend the supplier that meets all requirements at the lowest effective cost.

**Tab 2: Ponude dobavljača**

| Supplier | Price per unit | Lead time (days) | Defect rate | Minimum order (units) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | $4.20 | 10 | 1.5% | 5,000 |
| B | $3.90 | 16 | 1.2% | 8,000 |
| C | $4.00 | 12 | 2.5% | 3,000 |
| D | $4.35 | 7 | 0.8% | 12,000 |

**Tab 3: Memo iz Finansija**

**27.** Which supplier(s) meet all of Procurement's requirements?
